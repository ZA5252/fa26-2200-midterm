FACULTY: Class assignments requiring laser cutting should be facilitated by LAs, TAs, or instructors.

STUDENTS: Assistance with laser cutting should be coordinated by class LAs or TAs first, then "Lazor Wizards" if available.

EVERYBODY: Laser cutter access is separate from general BTU access and REQUIRES AN ORIENTATION.


Laser Cutter Orientations

The laser cutter orientation schedule can be found on the BTU Calendar page.

Anyone can gain access to operate the laser cutter using the process described in the following section.

Otherwise, schedule a time to complete your laser cutting job with one of the Lazor Wizards listed below or found on the BTU Staff page.


To gain full independent access to laser cutters:

  1. Complete the laser cutter orientation.
  2. As long as you need assistance, schedule laser appointments with a BTU Lazor Wizard.
  3. Lazor Wizards provide "guided access". We will help you as much, or as little as needed.
  4. When you demonstrate independent and proper use of the laser cutter, you will have full access.
  5. Block 1-hour times on the whiteboard next to each laser. (Not during scheduled orientations.)

BTU Lazor Wizards

Lazor Wizards are BTU Staff and Mischief Makers who are experts at operating laser cutters. They are available to help you with laser cutting projects.

If you need help with laser cutters, email one or more Lasor Wizards to get assistance.


Laser Cutting Equipment & Materials

  • 75 Watt CO2 Laser, 30" x 18" bed size [762mm x 457mm]
  • 75 Watt CO2 Laser, 40" x 28" bed size [1016mm x 711mm]
